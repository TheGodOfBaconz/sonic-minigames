My story with this fandom

I've been part of this fandom for about two years now, and honestly it's given me way more than just a favorite game series — it's how I've met some of my closest friends online.

This site is my way of putting that into something real: a place to talk about what I love, show off the characters that mean the most to me, and share a little fan game I made myself. If you're into SEGA too, I'd love for this to find its way to you.
